Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

OnlineLao Websolutions ("we") runs Vardaan's Blockland ("the Game"). The Game is made for children, so we collect as little as possible. This page explains what is stored and where.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up, no account, no name, no email, no phone number and no location from players.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no analytics trackers.

What stays on your device

The Game saves your world, characters, settings, parent PIN and play-time on the device and browser you use (browser local storage). This includes quest progress, stars and stickers, and any family photos a parent chooses to add to the Family Album. Photos are never uploaded; they stay on that device and we cannot see them. The weekly report for parents is also built from data kept only on the device. Pictures taken with the in-game photo mode are saved only if the player chooses to save or share them. Clearing browser data removes it.

Purchases

If a parent buys the Hero Pack, the payment is handled by Razorpay on its own secure checkout. Razorpay collects the payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store card, UPI or bank details. Our payment server only checks that the payment is genuine and returns an unlock code. It does not keep a customer database. Like most servers, it may briefly process technical information such as the IP address to protect against abuse.

Third-party services

  • Razorpay: payment processing, loaded only when a parent opens the purchase screen.
  • Google Fonts: the Game loads fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.
  • GitHub Pages and Railway: host the Game and the payment server.

Children

The Game is meant for children playing under a parent's supervision. Purchases are protected by a parent PIN. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can delete all saved Game data from your browser settings at any time. For any privacy question or request, email onlinelaoweb@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
